Career Path

What Does a Pilot's Career Actually Look Like After Training?

1

Junior First Officer (JFO)

Right after your CPL from DGCA, you join an airline as a JFO. It's a 6-month trainee stage with a stipend — your first real experience inside a commercial cockpit.

2

First Officer (Co-Pilot)

After JFO training, you become a co-pilot on large passenger aircraft — flying routes, assisting the Captain, and building your flying hours. Salary increases significantly here.

3

Obtain ATPL (Airline Transport Pilot License)

After 1,500 hours of flying (usually 3–4 years), you can apply for ATPL from DGCA. This is the license that makes you eligible to become a Captain.

4

Captain (Pilot-in-Command)

You're now in command of the aircraft, the crew, and the passengers. Highest responsibility. Highest pay. The goal every pilot works toward.

FAQs

Questions Parents & Students Ask Us

I am from a Commerce / Arts background. Can I still join an aviation course at SkyWize?
Yes, absolutely. You do not need Science to join most aviation courses at SkyWize. Cabin Crew Excellence Program, Airline Interview Preparation, and Drone Pilot Licensing are open to students from any stream — Science, Commerce, or Arts.
My child just passed Class 12. Is this the right time to join SkyWize?
Yes, right after Class 12 is actually the best time to start. Aviation careers have an age factor, especially for pilots. The earlier you begin your DGCA ground training, the faster you complete your exams and get into a flying school.
What is the DGCA exam and how hard is it to clear?
DGCA stands for Directorate General of Civil Aviation — India's official aviation authority. To become a licensed pilot in India, you must pass DGCA theory exams in subjects like Air Navigation, Meteorology, Air Regulations, Technical General, and RTR (Radio Telephony).
Are there age limits for becoming a pilot in India?
Yes. For CPL (Commercial Pilot License), you must be at least 17 years old to get a Student Pilot License, and at least 18 years old to obtain a CPL. Most airlines have an upper age limit of around 36–40 years for fresh pilot recruitment, though this varies.
What is a Drone Pilot License and why should my child consider it?
A Drone Pilot License is an official DGCA certification that allows you to fly commercial drones legally in India. It's the fastest-growing area in aviation, with opportunities in defense, agriculture, filmmaking, and logistics.
Is aviation a good career for girls? Are there enough opportunities?
Aviation is one of the best career choices for girls in India right now. Indian airlines are actively hiring, and there is a strong push to increase the number of female pilots in India.
Can I become a pilot if I wear glasses or have weak eyesight?
This depends on the degree of your vision problem. Mild refractive errors — nearsightedness or farsightedness within a certain range — are acceptable for a pilot license, provided the vision is fully correctable with glasses or contact lenses.
